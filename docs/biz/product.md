# Product direction

> updated 2026-09-01 · 0.1.0

Shadow Rift v1 exists to validate whether a compact dark-fantasy platform combat loop is fun on a phone before content or monetization expands.

- Primary player: mobile action-RPG players who want short, direct combat sessions.
- v1 value: responsive local movement, readable hit feedback, a two-hit combo, two distinct skills, light equipment progression, and a boss endpoint.
- Runtime: offline single-player Godot gameplay on Android and iOS with no gameplay-server dependency in v1.
- Validation: on-device players can finish the one-zone loop and want to replay it without needing quests, gacha, or a meta economy.
- Monetization: none in v1. Diamond, gacha, pity, IAP, drop-odds disclosure, restore purchases, analytics, accounts, cloud save, and competitive integrity remain later decisions.
